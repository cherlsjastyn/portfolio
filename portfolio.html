About me & what I can do

An IT student who learns by building things, breaking them, and then fixing them.

Over my studies I've built a good number of websites, apps, and full systems for school projects. Each one taught me how the pieces fit together: front end, back end, data, and the people who actually have to use the thing.

I'm not a coding genius, and I'd rather say that up front. I'd rate myself average at best when it comes to writing code from scratch. What I do well is understand the problem, use modern tools (including AI assistants) to move faster, then read, test, and adjust the result until it actually works. I think of AI as a very fast study buddy that still needs a human to check its homework.

What I'm aiming for: to get stronger in the fundamentals while building a career around testing, organizing, and connecting systems, not just writing code.

Outside the code: I enjoy learning how networks hold everything together, making messy data make sense, and keeping a project on track.
